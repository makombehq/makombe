# KubeVirt Summit Virtual

KubeVirt Summit is our annual online conference, now in its sixth year, in which the entire broader community meets to showcase all things KubeVirt, whether that is introducing new features, deploying in production, proposing architectural changes, or providing in-depth tutorials. This will be a very special Summit as we will be marking 10 years of KubeVirt!
