# OPI Summit on DPU/IPUs

The Second OPI Summit is open to everyone involved in the design, development, integration, marketing, use, or support of DPU/IPUs, related hardware, software, or services. The event will focus on improving further knowledge of DPU/IPUs, with emphasis on ease-of-use, PoC implementation and experimental results.
