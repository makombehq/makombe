# AGNTCon + MCPCon North America: The AI Agents Conference

AGNTCon + MCPCon is the flagship annual conference for the open agentic AI ecosystem. The event brings together the builders, contributors, and enterprises working across MCP and the broader landscape of agentic AI systems to share learnings, surface common challenges, and advance open, interoperable approaches to building AI agents at scale.
